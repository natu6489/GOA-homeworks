my favourite films

Dead Poets Society

Director: Peter Weir
Main Actors: Robin Williams, Robert Sean Leonard, Ethan Hawke
Genre: Drama
Release Year: 1989
IMDb Rating: 8.1/10

Story: The movie is about a teacher, Mr. Keating, who works at a boys’ school. He teaches his students to love
poetry and to think for themselves. He tells them to “seize the day,” which means to enjoy life and follow their dreams.
His lessons change the students’ lives forever.

Why I Like It: I love this film because it is very emotional and inspiring. Robin Williams is amazing in this role. The movie
teaches us to be brave, be different, and live life with passion.

The Phantom Of The Opera

Director: Joel Schumacher
Main Actors: Gerard Butler, Emmy Rossum, Patrick Wilson
Genre: Musical, Romance, Drama
Release Year: 2004
IMDb Rating: 7.2/10

Story:
The movie takes place in a big opera house in Paris. A young singer named Christine becomes famous,
but a mysterious man called the Phantom lives under the opera house and secretly helps her. He loves
her deeply, but his love becomes dangerous when she falls in love with another man.

Why I Like It:
I like this film because the music is beautiful and emotional. The story is sad but very romantic.
The costumes and sets are amazing, and the songs stay in your head for a long time.

Harry Potter

Director: Chris Columbus
Main Actors: Daniel Radcliffe, Emma Watson, Rupert Grint
Genre: Fantasy, Adventure
Release Year: 2001
IMDb Rating: 7.6/10

Story: Harry Potter is an orphan who finds out on his 11th birthday that he is a wizard.
He goes to a magic school called Hogwarts, where he makes new friends and learns spells. But he also
discovers that a dark wizard named Voldemort is trying to return to power.

Why I Like It: I like this movie because it is full of magic, friendship, and adventure. The world of Hogwarts
is amazing and fun to watch. It’s the start of a great story that continues in the other Harry Potter movies.
